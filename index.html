The case: Larkspur Pay

The labs run inside a fictional B2B payments company: $96M revenue, 420 staff in the US and Ireland, card data in scope for PCI DSS, an EU e-money entity under GDPR and DORA, a first SOC 2 Type II window opening, and an AI pilot launched without review.

Every dataset came with planted problems, the way evidence arrives on the job: a terminated admin still active in Okta, code merged without approval, a vendor report with exceptions, and a breach whose binding deadline was four hours, not 72.
