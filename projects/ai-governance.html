GRC project · Labs 10.1

AI Use-Case Governance under the EU AI Act

Assessed two AI use cases and split one into its two purposes: fraud detection (not high-risk) and credit limits (high-risk under Annex III).

EU AI ActNIST AI RMF 1.0ISO/IEC 42001

Skills shown

  • AI risk assessment
  • Regulatory classification
  • Acceptable use policy

Artifacts

  • Lab workbook (T_AI_Intake)

The situation

A support summarization tool went live without review, and product proposed a payee scoring model.

Approach

  1. Built an intake covering purpose, data, people affected, decisions and human oversight.
  2. Classified each use under the EU AI Act, with the current high-risk timeline checked.
  3. Mapped risks to NIST AI RMF functions and set required controls.
  4. Drafted a one-page generative AI acceptable use standard.

Analysis

Use caseClassificationDecision
Support CopilotNot high-risk; transparency dutyApprove with conditions
Payee scorer: fraud detectionExcluded from Annex III credit categoryApprove with monitoring
Payee scorer: credit limitsHigh-risk (creditworthiness of individuals)Separate model; conformity work first

Findings

  • Splitting a model by purpose changes its legal classification.
  • US fair-lending duties apply to credit decisions regardless of the AI Act.

Recommendations

  • Separate the two models; route the credit model through conformity assessment before the 2027 deadline.

Context: Larkspur Pay is a fictional company in the Hands-On GRC Training Program. All names and data are invented.
