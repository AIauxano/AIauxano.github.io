GRC project · Labs 8.1, 8.2

Business Impact Analysis and Ransomware Tabletop

Set recovery targets from business impact, exposed a 24-hour data-loss gap in the payouts platform, and facilitated a six-inject executive tabletop.

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3DORA resilience testingPCI DSS 12.10.2

Skills shown

  • BIA
  • RTO / RPO
  • Tabletop facilitation
  • After-action reporting

Artifacts

  • Lab workbook (T_BIA, T_Tabletop, T_AAR)

The situation

No recovery time objective existed for payouts, and the incident plan had never been exercised.

Approach

  1. Interviewed process owners for impact at 1, 8, 24 and 72 hours.
  2. Set MTPD, RTO and RPO and compared against current capability.
  3. Designed a ransomware scenario starting at the outsourced support vendor, with six timed injects.
  4. Ran a hot wash and wrote an after-action report with owned, dated actions.

Analysis

ProcessRTO targetRPO targetToday
Payout processing4hNear zeroRPO up to 24h; restores untested
Card authorization4hZeroSame gap
PayrollDays24hAdequate

Findings

  • Daily snapshots cannot meet payouts' recovery point; the gap feeds risk R-01.
  • Nobody could say who has authority to halt payouts.

Recommendations

  • Add halt authority to the IR decision table.
  • Protect payouts and card authorization first; payroll can wait.
