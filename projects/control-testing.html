Control Testing: Offboarding and Change Management

Tested two key controls the way an auditor would, found a terminated IT admin still active and code merged without approval, and caught an incomplete population before relying on it.

SOC 2 CC6.2, CC8.1PCI DSS 6.5.1, 8.2.5ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.18, A.8.32

Skills shown

  • Audit sampling
  • Population completeness
  • Workpapers

Artifacts

  • Termination report (CSV)
  • Okta export (CSV)
  • Change sample (CSV)
  • Lab workbook (T_Test_Offboarding, T_Test_Change)

The situation

Two controls had to work for the SOC 2 window: access removed within one business day of termination, and every production change approved by someone other than the author before merge.

Approach

  1. Reconciled the HR termination report to the Okta export before sampling.
  2. Tested all 10 October terminations (small population), counting business-day lag and logins after termination.
  3. Confirmed the change population (1,840 merges) against the deploy log, then tested 15 merges on four attributes.
  4. Wrote workpapers with procedure, results, exceptions, conclusion and root cause.

Findings

  • Offboarding: 3 of 10 exceptions (30%) plus an incomplete population. Not effective.
  • Change management: 4 of 15 exceptions (27%); three involve admin bypass. Not effective.
  • Root causes are design problems (a manual email handoff, unrestricted bypass), so reminders will not fix them.

Recommendations

  • Connect Workday to Okta for same-day automated deprovisioning; bring contractors into Workday.
  • Restrict branch-protection bypass and alert on every use.
  • Run a lookback on what the late-removed accounts accessed.
