ISO 27001 Gap Assessment and Statement of Applicability

Rated ten Annex A controls on evidence, ranked the gaps that matter before a SOC 2 window opens, and built a 93-control Statement of Applicability with defensible exclusions.

Artifacts

  • Lab workbook (T_GapAssessment, T_SoA)

The situation

The SOC 2 Type II observation window opens January 1. Leadership needed to know which control gaps would show up as audit exceptions in the first months.

Approach

  1. Rated maturity 0-5 using only documented evidence.
  2. Set June 2027 targets and calculated gaps.
  3. Prioritized by impact on the audit window and assigned owners and effort.
  4. Decided applicability for all 93 Annex A controls with sourced justifications, avoiding common traps such as excluding physical controls for a cloud company.

Findings

  • Most controls sit at 1 or 2: done, but not documented or consistent.
  • Five gaps matter before January: change bypass, log review, offboarding, restore testing and vulnerability SLAs.

Recommendations

  • Fix the five gaps first; each maps to a SOC 2 common criterion the auditor will sample early.
