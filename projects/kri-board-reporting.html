KRI Dashboard and Board Risk Memo

Designed key risk indicators tied to risk appetite and wrote a two-page Audit and Risk Committee memo that leads with decisions.

NIST CSF 2.0 GovernRisk appetite

Skills shown

  • Dashboards

Artifacts

The situation

The board chair asked for a cyber risk briefing: what could hurt the company and whether management has it in hand.

Approach

  1. Defined KRIs with formula, source, owner and thresholds tied to appetite.
  2. Charted six months of data with automatic red, amber and green status.
  3. Wrote the memo: bottom line, top five risks, assurance calendar, decisions requested.

Analysis

Critical vulns past SLA41%22%Improving, still amber
Offboarding within SLA58%70%Stuck: design problem

Findings

  • Offboarding will not move until the HR-to-identity integration ships; the memo says so plainly.
  • Phishing click rate alone is a weak KRI; reporting rate is a better signal.

Recommendations

  • Ask the committee to approve the $180k resilience spend using the FAIR figures.
